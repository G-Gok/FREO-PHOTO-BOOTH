# FREO Sticker AI Prompts (Costume & Hats Collection)

Use these prompts in AI image generators (like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion) to create transparent PNG stickers for the FREO Photo Booth. 

**General Style Tip:** For best results, add "--no background" or "isolated on white background" to ensure easy removal of the background.

---

### 1. The Lemon Beret (Classic Chic)
> **Prompt:** A high-quality digital sticker of a stylish French beret made entirely from a giant, textured lemon slice. Bright yellow, with a small green leaf as the "stem" on top. Flat 2D vector style, clean thick outlines, vibrant colors, isolated on plain white background, high resolution.

### 2. The Banana Peel Beanie (Streetwear Vibe)
> **Prompt:** A fun streetwear-style beanie hat designed to look like an upside-down banana peel. The "peels" drape down slightly like flaps. Bright yellow with subtle brown spots for realism. Minimalist sticker design, white border, flat shading, isolated on white background.

### 3. The Papaya Flower Crown (Tropical Queen)
> **Prompt:** A tropical headband crown featuring vibrant orange papaya slices and exotic tropical flowers. Lush green leaves in the background. Highly detailed digital sticker, bold colors, 2D illustrative style, isolated on white background.

### 4. The "Fresh" Sun Visor (Active Lifestyle)
> **Prompt:** A modern translucent green sun visor hat with the "FREO" logo on the front. Water droplets on the surface to signify "Freshness". Clean vector art, sportswear aesthetic, bright and airy, isolated on white background.

### 5. The Fruit Basket Top Hat (Whimsical/Party)
> **Prompt:** A whimsical top hat that looks like a woven wicker basket overflowing with lemons, bananas, and papayas. Cartoon sticker style, cheerful and colorful, thick line art, isolated on white background.

### 6. The Tropical Leaf Explorer Hat (Adventure)
> **Prompt:** A wide-brimmed safari explorer hat covered in a pattern of large Monstera and tropical palm leaves. Natural earth tones mixed with vibrant green. High-quality sticker design, flat 2D art, isolated on white background.

### 7. The FREO Captain's Cap (Authority)
> **Prompt:** A crisp white captain's hat with a gold rope detail. Instead of an anchor, the front emblem is a minimalist icon of a lemon and a banana crossed like swords. Professional vector sticker, clean edges, nautical theme, isolated on white background.

---

### Pro-Tip for Transparent Background:
After generating these images, use a tool like **Adobe Express (Remove Background)** or **remove.bg** to get the final transparent PNGs for the website. 
Then, replace the placeholder links in `index.html` (e.g., `graduation-cap.png`) with your newly hosted image URLs!
